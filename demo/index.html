THE CONSTRUCT home screen demo

A Matrix-style home screen built for a Sony Google TV, running here in your browser. Your keyboard is the remote; on a phone or tablet, use the remote under the screen. Apps and shows are samples; nothing here reaches a real TV.

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger screen.

Stuck in something? Back gets you out: Esc or Backspace on a keyboard, or the BACK button on the on-screen remote. It closes the console, stops a fight or The Last Stand, and wakes the screensaver.

RUN IT NOW

In The Last Stand you fight: hit each arrow as it flashes up to land your blow or block his, press → to charge in the sky, tap Enter to get back up, and press Enter at the end when it's time. About two and a half minutes; Esc stops it. On the TV these all happen on their own (see below). The screensaver plays a fight every 30 seconds after the rain spells the time.

ON ITS OWN, AND THE TWO RED EVENTS

It happens by itself. Leave the home on and the moments turn up at random, every 5 to 10 minutes. An agent alert and the proximity alarm take turns, one every 9 minutes. They pause while an app or the screensaver is on. The buttons above and the console just run them now instead of waiting.

After a fight. Wake the screensaver during a fight, or finish a program in the console, and the command line answers with a line from that scene.

1. Wake up
The construct wakes up: the screen is taken over, the glass shatters and everything turns red. System compromised. In red, agents come every 1 to 2 minutes instead of every 18.
2. The pill choice
Morpheus offers the choice. Blue takes you back to the green. Red: the mirror takes you, then the construct breaks apart, with tearing, errors and flickering lights. Run the pill choice again and take blue to put it back together.
